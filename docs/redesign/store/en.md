## Description (1849/4000)

Every stage, you get 7 picks. Every pick, you choose one of two cards.

Each card shows its Danger right on its face: 1 to 4 fuse segments. More segments means a bigger chance to blow up, and a bigger payout. Play it safe or gamble on a big multiplier? Every card is a small trade-off, and no choice is best every time.

BOOM ONLY COSTS ONE PICK
A bomb takes one pick, not your whole run. Reach the target score to clear the stage and turn the picks you have left into coins, or push on to the Stretch goal for a bonus. Safe picks in a row build a Streak that boosts your score and carries into the next stage, until one boom resets it.

SIX STAGES, YOUR OWN PLAYSTYLE
- Spend coins in the shop between stages on 10 Blessings and 4 items
- A Shield blocks a boom on its own; Peek, Reroll and Double are yours to time
- Stages 2, 4 and 6 have a Boss (Fog, Crush, Shaky...), and every other stage has its own rule
- Carry up to 5 Blessings; every combination plays differently

DAILY CHALLENGE
One deck a day, the same cards and rules for everyone. The first try is tough, but you can retry as often as you like: the same spot always deals the same card, so remembering the results takes you further each time. Your record is which try won, and you can share your result grid with friends.

KEEP GOING AFTER YOU WIN
- Endless stages with a Boss every 2 stages: how far can you get?
- Six difficulties, each unlocked by winning the one before, and the rules stack
- Free play: start a run any time and try a new idea

MY RECORDS
Best score, longest streak, most stages cleared, a Daily Challenge calendar, win rates per difficulty and per Blessing. Every number has a next milestone to chase, and you're told the moment you break a record.

Easy to learn, playable one-handed, a few minutes per run. How strong is your intuition, really?

Contains ads.
